Install LapLogic

Plug the board in with a USB data cable, then click Connect & install and pick its port. It's often listed as USB Serial, CH340, CP210x or USB JTAG/serial debug unit.

If it won't connect, use boot mode: press and hold BOOT while you click Connect & install, keep holding, and let go when you see Erasing or Installing.

Connecting...
Board won't connect? Use boot mode

Most boards switch into boot mode by themselves. If yours doesn't, hold its BOOT button while you connect.

  1. Press and hold BOOT.
  2. While holding it, click Connect & install and pick the port.
  3. Keep holding until the progress bar says Erasing or Installing, then let go.

Still won't connect? Unplug the board, hold BOOT while you plug it back in, then click Connect & install. Ticking Safe speed also helps with long cables.

If the screen stays blank after installing, press RST once (or unplug and replug).

Connect to Wi-Fi

LapLogic restarts after installing. Its Wi-Fi details are sent over the same USB cable. Prefer your phone? Skip this step and scan the QR code on the screen instead.

2.4 GHz networks only. If your router has separate 2.4 and 5 GHz names, pick the 2.4 GHz one.

Settings

optional

Favourite driver, time zone, screen orientation, auto scroll and more, set over the same USB cable. You can change these later from any phone or PC on your Wi-Fi at laplogic.local.

Before you start

  • Use Chrome or Edge on a Mac, Windows or Linux computer.
  • Use a USB data cable; many cables only charge.
  • Close the Arduino IDE serial monitor or anything else using the board.
  • Board won't connect? Hold BOOT while you click Connect & install, and let go when you see Erasing or Installing.
