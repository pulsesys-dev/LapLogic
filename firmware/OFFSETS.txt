LapLogic b1.7.1 firmware

Easiest: flash the merged file for your board at offset 0x0.
  LapLogic-Guition-JC4827W543.bin   Guition JC4827W543C (ESP32-S3, 4MB flash, 8MB OPI PSRAM)
  LapLogic-CYD-ESP32-2432S028R.bin  ESP32-2432S028R Cheap Yellow Display (ESP32, 4MB flash)

esptool examples:
  esptool.py --chip esp32s3 --baud 921600 write_flash 0x0 LapLogic-Guition-JC4827W543.bin
  esptool.py --chip esp32   --baud 921600 write_flash 0x0 LapLogic-CYD-ESP32-2432S028R.bin

Separate parts, if your tool needs them (note the bootloader address differs):

  guition-jc4827w543/            cyd-esp32-2432s028r/
  0x0000   bootloader            0x1000   bootloader
  0x8000   partitions            0x8000   partitions
  0xE000   boot_app0.bin         0xE000   boot_app0.bin
  0x10000  LapLogic.ino.bin       0x10000  LapLogic.ino.bin

Built with ESP32 Arduino core 2.0.17, GFX Library for Arduino 1.4.9,
LVGL 8.3.11, ArduinoJson 7.2.1, WiFiManager 2.0.17, PubSubClient 2.8.
